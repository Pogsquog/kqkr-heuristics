    What to watch for

    • The box. The queen fences the black king into a shrinking area near an edge. Turn on “Black king's box” and watch it shrink while the white king walks up.
    • Rook near its king. A rook far from its king gets forked by a queen check. Black keeps it one step away, so White works to separate them.
    • Queen on the king's line. In the tuned heuristic, the queen sharing a rank or file with the black king was one of the strongest signals.
    • Stalemate trap. If the black king has no moves, the rook can sacrifice itself with endless checks. Load “Stalemate trap”: Qc2 looks natural but draws.
    • Two counts. “Mate” is the fastest mate. “Win rook” is the fastest safe capture of the rook, after which the queen mates alone. Optimal play often looks different under each.
